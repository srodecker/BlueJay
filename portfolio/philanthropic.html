01  ·  Philanthropic

Camp, charity and congregation.

The property spent its first fifty-nine years as organised group accommodation — a railway camp, then a lodge. Philanthropic use is therefore the least novel thing that could happen here, which is exactly its advantage: it asks the county to permit something the site has already demonstrably been.

File RR–2026–01/01 Extent 14.16 ha · 35 ac Concepts 4 Status Concept review

Concepts

Four philanthropic routes, ordered by how closely they track the existing use history.

Youth & Summer Camp

Priority

A direct return to the property’s original function. Seasonal residential camp for a youth, scouting or educational organisation, using the historic clearing as the core and the stonework as its assembly point. Sleeping capacity, water and waste are the governing constraints, not land area.

  • PrecedentPacific Electric Railway Camp, 1915–1941
  • SeasonSummer, with shoulder-season group hire
  • EntitlementOrganisational camp use — county permit path To be confirmed
  • CapitalTo be modelled
  • CounterpartyCamp operator, scouting body or school district

Religious Retreat & Conference Grounds

Priority

Year-round retreat and conference grounds for a church, diocese or faith-based organisation. Mountain retreat centres in the San Bernardino range are long established and frequently held in perpetuity, which suits a seller looking for a permanent outcome rather than a resale.

  • Use patternYear-round weekend and week-long retreats
  • BuildingsAssembly hall, dormitory or cabin cluster, refectory
  • EntitlementAssembly use — county permit path To be confirmed
  • CapitalTo be modelled
  • CounterpartyDenominational body or independent congregation

Charitable Ownership & Outdoor Education

Concept

Transfer to a charitable foundation operating the property as an outdoor education and field-study site. Lower built intensity than a residential camp: day programmes, a field classroom, interpretive use of the 1917 stonework as a documented historic feature.

  • Built intensityLow — day use with minimal overnight
  • Heritage angleStonework as interpreted historic feature
  • EntitlementLightest of the four philanthropic routes
  • CapitalTo be modelled
  • CounterpartyEnvironmental or educational foundation

Conservation Partnership

Concept

Conservation easement or fee transfer to a land trust over all or part of the holding. Worth modelling even if it is not the chosen outcome: it establishes a floor value, and a partial easement over the steepest or most sensitive ground can make the remainder considerably easier to entitle.

  • StructureFull fee, bargain sale, or partial easement
  • Strategic valueCan de-risk entitlement on the retained parcel
  • Tax treatmentTo be modelled
  • ValuationTo be modelled
  • CounterpartyRegional or statewide land trust
Why this category leads. A non-profit or religious counterparty typically brings a longer time horizon, a lower return threshold and considerably more community goodwill than a for-profit applicant. Where an entitlement is contested, the identity of the applicant is frequently the deciding variable. Tax treatment of a donation or bargain sale should be modelled alongside a straight disposal before this category is ranked.
