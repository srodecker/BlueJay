04  ·  Recreational

The lightest touch on the ground.

Recreational use asks less of the land than anything else in this file and returns revenue sooner. It is also the most reversible: nothing here forecloses a development or philanthropic outcome later, which makes this category a credible interim position as much as a destination.

File RR–2026–01/04 Extent 14.16 ha · 35 ac Concepts 4 Status Concept review

Concepts

Four recreational concepts, all reversible, all seasonal.

Glamping

Priority

Furnished tents, domes or cabin tents on prepared platforms, sited among the timber and along the water. Low capital, largely demountable, and directly aligned with the qualities the land already has — old trees, running water and standing stone. The nearest thing in the file to revenue in a single season.

  • Build requiredPlatforms, sanitation, potable water, access track
  • ReversibilityHigh — substantially demountable
  • SeasonLate spring to early autumn
  • EntitlementUse permit; occupancy and sanitation conditions
  • UnitsTo be modelled
  • CapitalLowest in the file — to be modelled

Seasonal Campground & Day Use

Concept

Conventional seasonal campground with designated sites, or gated day use for groups. The simplest possible commercial relationship with the land: access, sanitation and supervision, and very little else. Lower rate per night than glamping, lower capital still.

  • Build requiredSites, sanitation, potable water, supervision
  • ReversibilityVery high
  • SeasonSummer
  • RateLower than glamping
  • CapitalTo be modelled

Trails & Stream Access

Concept

A maintained trail system through the timber and along the seasonal watercourses, whether as a standalone membership or day-permit offer or as the amenity layer underneath any other concept in this file. Rarely the whole answer; frequently a component of it.

  • FunctionStandalone, or amenity beneath another use
  • Build requiredTrail cutting, waymarking, ongoing maintenance
  • ReversibilityVery high
  • RevenueModest on its own — to be modelled
  • CapitalVery low

Group Retreat Hire

Study

Whole-site hire to corporate, wellness or affinity groups on a per-weekend basis. Sits naturally on top of glamping infrastructure and lifts the yield from it considerably, at the cost of a more demanding standard of service and a smaller booking pool.

  • ModelWhole-site exclusive hire, per weekend
  • DependencyBuilt on glamping infrastructure
  • YieldHigher per night than individual bookings
  • Service standardMaterially more demanding
  • RateTo be modelled
Consider this as an interim position. Glamping and seasonal camping can generate income while the longer entitlement questions are answered, without committing the land to anything. A season of operation also produces something no study can: real occupancy, real access and real seasonality data to underwrite the larger decision with.
