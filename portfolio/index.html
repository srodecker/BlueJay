Rivosus Ranch: position and potential.

Fourteen and a sixth hectares of forested mountain land in the woods surrounding Lake Arrowhead, between Blue Jay and Agua Fria. The following pages set out the uses under consideration for the property, grouped into four categories, together with the supporting file archive.

File RR–2026–01 Extent 14.16 ha · 35 ac County San Bernardino, CA Status Concept review

Summary

What the land is, stated plainly.

The asset

14.16 hectares (approximately 35 acres) of mature timber, needle floor and seasonal watercourses. A clearing at the centre carries the surviving stonework of the 1915 Pacific Electric Railway Camp and the 1944 Pine View Lodge.

The position

Inside the Lake Arrowhead community band, between Blue Jay and Agua Fria. Established mountain resort catchment with existing year-round population, seasonal visitor demand and constrained developable supply.

The lever

A hundred and ten years of documented, continuous, permitted occupation — camp, then lodge. The site has a use history, not a blank record, and the stonework is its physical evidence.

Diligence is outstanding. Parcel numbers, zoning and land-use designation, slope and buildable area, access and easements, utility service, water rights, septic feasibility, fire-hazard classification and title are all still to be confirmed. Every figure on the following pages is marked accordingly. Nothing here has been modelled against verified constraints yet.
