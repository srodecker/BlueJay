02  ·  Development

Lots, roofs, and the long entitlement.

The highest ceiling in the file and, without question, the heaviest lift. Mountain subdivision in San Bernardino County is slow, expensive and publicly contested, and nothing in this category should be underwritten before slope, access, water and fire classification are known quantities.

File RR–2026–01/02 Extent 14.16 ha · 35 ac Concepts 4 Status Concept review

Concepts

Four development positions, from a full subdivision to holding the land whole.

Residential Lots

Priority

Subdivision of the holding into individual residential parcels for sale to builders or end users. The conventional highest-and-best-use answer for 35 acres inside an established mountain community, and the conventional answer is not always wrong. Yield is entirely a function of buildable area after slope and required access.

  • YieldTo be modelled (pending slope and access)
  • Buildable areaTo be confirmed
  • EntitlementTentative and final map — multi-year
  • EnvironmentalCEQA review; a public process by design
  • CapitalHighest in the file — to be modelled
  • HorizonLong

Affordable & Workforce Housing

Priority

Deed-restricted affordable or workforce housing. The mountain communities run a genuine structural shortage of housing for the people who staff them, and that shortage is politically legible in a way that market-rate subdivision is not. Density bonus and streamlining provisions may materially shorten the approval path.

  • DriverDocumented workforce shortage in the resort band
  • MechanismsDensity bonus; streamlining; tax credit equity
  • EntitlementPotentially faster than market-rate
  • Subsidy stackTo be modelled
  • CapitalTo be modelled
  • CounterpartyAffordable housing developer or authority

Clustered Cabin Court

Concept

A small number of cabins grouped tightly on the least sensitive ground, with the balance of the acreage left in open space. Lower unit count than a full subdivision, but far less road, far less grading, and a far more defensible story in a public hearing.

  • FootprintConcentrated — majority of site undisturbed
  • InfrastructureMaterially lower road and grading cost
  • EntitlementEasier to defend publicly than full subdivision
  • YieldTo be modelled
  • CapitalTo be modelled

Hold Whole — Single Compound

Study

Retain the parcel intact as a single private estate or family compound, restoring the stonework as its centrepiece. The lowest-intensity outcome and the baseline every other concept should be measured against. If no concept beats holding it whole on a risk-adjusted basis, that is itself a finding.

  • FunctionBaseline case for comparison
  • EntitlementMinimal — single residence
  • HeritageStonework restored as the centrepiece
  • ValueTo be modelled
  • HorizonIndefinite
Sequence this category last. Every concept below depends on the same four unknowns: buildable area after slope, legal and physical access, water and septic capacity, and fire-hazard severity classification. Until those four are answered, yield assumptions are guesses. Commission the constraints work before the pro forma, not after.
